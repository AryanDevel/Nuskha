First release. A prescription's Clinical IR compiles to an ABDM PrescriptionRecord document bundle that passes the HL7 validator against `ndhm.in#6.5.0`. `nuskha compile` and `nuskha validate` do it from the command line. No model, OCR or terminology coding yet.
